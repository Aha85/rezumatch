# Hebrew and the Israeli market

## Which language
- The document follows the listing: English listing = English resume and letter, even if the conversation is in Hebrew.
- Many Israeli tech and international companies post in English and expect English resumes.
- Hebrew resumes: public sector, local companies, Hebrew-only listings.

## Hebrew layout (templates handle it)
- `<html lang="he" dir="rtl">`. Everything mirrors: name and contact at the right, bullets start at the right.
- No letter spacing on Hebrew headings (it splits the letters apart).
- Phone numbers, emails, URLs, dates and product names stay left-to-right (`.contact a`, `.dates`, `<bdi>`).
- Dates: years only, "2021 - 2023". Current: "2025 - היום".
- Headings: תקציר, כישורים, ניסיון תעסוקתי, פרויקטים, השכלה, שירות צבאי.
- English terms inside Hebrew text as they are (Python, CRM), no transliteration.

## Israeli norms
- Military service: include when relevant to the role (officer, tech units, management) or when it fills a gap. Junior roles: one line or omit for senior candidates.
- Degrees: B.A. / B.Sc. / M.A. / MBA, institution, years.
- No ID number, photo, marital status or address beyond the city.
- Phone in international format for English resumes: +972-5X-XXX-XXXX. Local format for Hebrew: 05X-XXX-XXXX.

## Hebrew writing
- Short, spoken Hebrew. Avoid translated phrasing that no Israeli would say out loud.
- Same rules as writing-quality.md: no em dashes, no slogans, one idea per bullet.
- File names: "<שם מלא> קורות חיים.pdf" or the English pattern, the user chooses.
