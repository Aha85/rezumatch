# Product: RezuMatch

**What it is:** a free Claude plugin and skill that fits a job seeker's resume (and cover letter) to one job listing, step by step, with an estimated ATS score. Never invents facts. English and Hebrew.

**Who it's for:** the general public looking for a job, including the least technical people. Many have never installed a plugin, opened a terminal or heard of an ATS.

**Made by:** Danny Shaket, AI trainer and MBA lecturer (dannyshaket.com). The tool is free; it also introduces people to Danny's work.

**Surfaces:**
- Public install and usage guide: https://www.dannyshaket.com/creations/rezumatch (source lives in the site repo, `scripts/creations/rezumatch-source.html`). Mode: Persuade, with a Read core (install steps).
- The plugin itself runs inside Claude (chat UI), not ours to design.

**Voice:** direct, warm, practical; plain spoken words; numbers over adjectives; no em dashes; no hype words. Hebrew addresses the reader as "אתם".

**Visual authority:** Danny's design system "Danny Presentation Dark EN/HEB" (navy ground, chalk text, one magenta accent, Assistant 800 headlines, hairline rules, square corners, no shadows or gradients).

**Assumptions (from the brief, not an interview):** dark version chosen by Danny for this page; success = a non-technical visitor installs in under 10 minutes without help, and some visitors continue to dannyshaket.com.
